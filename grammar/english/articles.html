01 Core Rules

Rule 1 Indefinite Articles (A / An)

Used before singular countable nouns when referring to something general or mentioned for the first time.

  • I saw a dog in the park.
  • She ate an apple.
Rule 2 The Sound Rule

The choice between a and an depends on the initial sound of the following word, not the initial letter.

  • Use a before a consonant sound: a book, a car, a university, a European, a one-rupee coin.
  • Use an before a vowel sound: an apple, an hour, an honest man, an MBA, an SOS.
Rule 3 Definite Article (The)

Used when the noun is specific, already known or unique.

RuleExample
Second mentionI saw a dog. The dog was black.
Unique objectsThe sun, the moon, the earth
SuperlativesThe best, the tallest
OrdinalsThe first, the second
Rivers, Seas, OceansThe Nile, the Pacific
Mountain rangesThe Himalayas
Countries with plural names or republicsThe USA, the UK, the Netherlands
Holy booksThe Bible, the Quran, the Gita
Musical instrumentsPlay the guitar
Parts of the bodyHit him on the head
Rule 4 Zero Article (No Article)

Used for general plural nouns, uncountable nouns and certain specific categories.

CategoryExample
Plural nouns (general)Books are useful.
Uncountable nouns (general)Water is life.

Rule 6 Fixed Expressions
  • In the morning, in the afternoon, in the evening
  • On the way, at the moment, in the end, on the other hand
  • Go to bed, go to work, go home

02 Context Clues and Sentence Logic

Determining the correct article requires analyzing the noun and its context within the sentence.

Specificity

Is the noun known to the reader or listener? If it has been mentioned before or is unique, use the. If it is one of many, use a/an.

  • He bought a car. The car is red.

Countability

Is the noun countable? If it is an uncountable noun used in a general sense, use the zero article. If it is a singular countable noun, use a/an.

  • Water is essential. (Uncountable general)
  • I need a glass of water. (Countable specific)

Sound vs Letter

Examine the pronunciation of the word immediately following the article. Words beginning with a silent h take an (an hour). Words beginning with u or eu that produce a y sound take a (a university).

03 SEE Appeared Questions

Question 1
A man came into (a/ an/ the/ no article) wood one day.
Answer: a
Explanation: Wood is a singular countable noun mentioned for the first time. It begins with a consonant sound.
Question 2
Once a chief decided to give (the/ an/ a/ no article) feast.
Answer: a
Explanation: Feast is a singular countable noun introduced for the first time.
Question 3
(a/ an/ the/ nothing) fox thought of a plan.
Answer: the
Explanation: The fox was already introduced in the previous sentence. It is a subsequent mention.
Question 4
There lived (a/ an/ the/ no article) elderly man.
Answer: an
Explanation: Elderly begins with a vowel sound. The noun is introduced for the first time.
Question 5
It is (a/ an/ the/ no article) UNESCO World Heritage Site.
Answer: a
Explanation: UNESCO is pronounced with a consonant sound "yu". Therefore "a" is used.
Question 6
There was once (an/ the/ a/ nothing) young boy.
Answer: a
Explanation: Young begins with a consonant sound. The noun is introduced for the first time.
Question 7
Everybody wants to be successful in (a/ an/ the/ no article) world.
Answer: the
Explanation: World is a unique entity in this context.
Question 8
There lived (a/ an/ the/ no article) girl near my house.
Answer: a
Explanation: Girl is a singular countable noun introduced for the first time.
Question 9
A man lived in (a/ an/ the/ no article) village long ago.
Answer: a
Explanation: Village is a singular countable noun introduced for the first time.
Question 10
Getting up early is one of (a/ an/ the/ no article) healthy habits.
Answer: the
Explanation: The phrase "one of the" is followed by a plural noun and a specific adjective requiring the definite article.

04 Common Mistakes to Avoid

I saw a dog. A dog was black. I saw a dog. The dog was black. Second mention requires "the".
He is an university student. He is a university student. University begins with a "yu" sound.
She waited for a hour. She waited for an hour. Hour begins with a silent "h".
The honesty is the best policy. Honesty is the best policy. Abstract nouns in a general sense take no article.
I like the music. I like music. General uncountable nouns take no article.
He plays the cricket. He plays cricket. Games take no article.
She has the headache. She has a headache. Singular countable nouns take a/an.
I go to the school to study. I go to school to study. Using the facility for its purpose takes no article.
He is best student. He is the best student. Superlatives require "the".
