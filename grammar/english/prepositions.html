Rule 4 Agent and Instrument
  • By is used for the agent (the doer). Example: written by Ram.
  • With is used for the instrument (the tool). Example: cut with a knife.

02 Context Clues and Sentence Logic

Identifying the correct preposition requires recognizing fixed expressions and understanding the spatial or temporal relationship in the sentence.

Fixed Expressions

Many prepositions do not follow logical rules but are fixed to specific words. Memorize these as chunks.

  • You are not interested on a topic, you are interested in it.
  • You are not good in a subject, you are good at it.

Spatial vs Temporal Logic

Determine if the phrase indicates location or time.

  • In refers to a large area or an enclosed space (in Nepal, in the box).
  • On refers to a surface (on the table).
  • At refers to a point (at the gate).

03 SEE Appeared Questions

Question 1
The tree was (with/ off/ by/ on) kind nature.
Answer: by
Explanation: By nature is a fixed expression meaning inherently.
Question 2
People are not interested (at/ on/ in/ for) it.
Answer: in
Explanation: The adjective interested is always followed by the preposition in.
Question 3
Visitors are amazed (at/ by/ on/ in) the sight.
Answer: by
Explanation: The adjective amazed is followed by the preposition by.
Question 4
They were accompanied (in/ on/ at/ from) Mr. Regmi.
Answer: by
Explanation: The verb accompanied is followed by the preposition by.
Question 5
The three gentlemen noticed the condition (in/ on/ at/ of) their new society.
Answer: of
Explanation: The noun condition is followed by the preposition of.
Question 6
He is good (at/ in/ on/ for) studies.
Answer: at
Explanation: The adjective good is always followed by the preposition at.
Question 7
She is afraid (from/ of/ with/ at) dogs.
Answer: of
Explanation: The adjective afraid is always followed by the preposition of.
Question 8
It depends (in/ on/ at/ for) you.
Answer: on
Explanation: The verb depend is always followed by the preposition on.
Question 9
He died (from/ of/ with/ by) cancer.
Answer: of
Explanation: The verb die is followed by the preposition of when referring to disease.
Question 10
I arrived (at/ in/ on/ to) Kathmandu.
Answer: in
Explanation: Arrive in is used for large places like cities and countries.

04 Common Mistakes to Avoid

I am interested on music. I am interested in music. Fixed expression.
She is good in maths. She is good at maths. Fixed expression.
He is afraid from dogs. He is afraid of dogs. Fixed expression.
I am proud for you. I am proud of you. Fixed expression.
It depends in you. It depends on you. Fixed expression.
Listen me. Listen to me. Fixed expression.
Wait me. Wait for me. Fixed expression.
He died from cancer. He died of cancer. Fixed expression.
I arrived to Kathmandu. I arrived in Kathmandu. Arrive in for large places.
I arrived in the station. I arrived at the station. Arrive at for small places.
on the morning in the morning Fixed expression.
in Monday on Monday Fixed expression.
in night at night Fixed expression.
