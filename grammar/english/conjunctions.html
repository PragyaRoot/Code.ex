01 Core Rules

Coordinating Conjunctions

These join words phrases or independent clauses of equal grammatical rank. The standard list is remembered by the acronym FANBOYS: For And Nor But Or Yet So.

ConjunctionFunctionExample
AndAddition or ConsequenceRam and Shyam are friends. Work hard and you will succeed.
ButContrastHe is poor but honest.
OrChoice or WarningTea or coffee. Hurry up or you will miss the bus.
NorNegative additionHe does not smoke nor does he drink.
ForReasonHe was punished for he was late.
SoResultIt was raining so we stayed home.
YetContrastHe is rich yet he is unhappy.

Subordinating Conjunctions

These join a dependent clause to a main clause establishing relationships such as cause time condition and purpose.

ConjunctionFunctionExample
BecauseReasonHe passed because he studied hard.
Although / ThoughContrastAlthough he was tired he kept working.
IfConditionIf it rains we will stay home.
UnlessNegative conditionUnless you study you will fail.
WhenTimeWhen I arrived he was sleeping.
WhileTime (simultaneous)While I was reading he came.
AfterTime (later)After he left I arrived.
BeforeTime (earlier)Before I came he had left.
UntilTime (up to)Wait until I return.
SinceTime or ReasonSince you are here let us start.
So thatPurposeHe studied hard so that he could pass.

Correlative Conjunctions and Advanced Structures

These work in pairs or specific structures to join balanced grammatical elements.

PairFunctionExample
Either...orChoice between twoEither Ram or Shyam will come.
Neither...norNegative of bothNeither Ram nor Shyam came.
Both...andAddition of twoBoth Ram and Shyam are here.
Not only...but alsoAddition with emphasisHe is not only smart but also kind.
No sooner...thanImmediate sequenceNo sooner had I reached the station than the train left.
Hardly...whenImmediate sequenceHardly had he started the car when the engine stopped.
Scarcely...whenImmediate sequenceScarcely had she entered the room when the phone rang.
So...thatResult with adjectiveThe box was so heavy that I could not lift it.
Such...thatResult with noun phraseIt was such a cold day that we stayed indoors.

Conjunctive Adverbs versus Conjunctions

Words like however therefore and consequently are adverbs not conjunctions. They cannot join two independent clauses with a comma. They require a semicolon or a period.

IncorrectCorrect
He was tired, however he kept working.He was tired. However, he kept working.
It was raining, therefore we stayed home.It was raining. Therefore, we stayed home.

02 Context Clues and Sentence Logic

Determining the correct conjunction requires analyzing the logical relationship between the two clauses.

Cause and Effect

If one clause explains why the other happened use because or since. If one clause shows the result of the other use so or therefore.

  • He was late because the bus broke down.
  • The bus broke down so he was late.

Contrast

If the two clauses present opposing ideas use although though but or however.

  • Although he studied hard he failed the exam.
  • He studied hard but he failed the exam.

Purpose

If one clause explains the intention behind the action in the other clause use so that.

  • She woke up early so that she could catch the train.

Condition

If one clause depends on the fulfillment of the other use if or unless.

  • You will pass if you study.
  • You will fail unless you study.

Correlative Logic

  • No sooner is always followed by than.
  • Hardly and Scarcely are always followed by when.
  • Such is followed by a noun phrase while So is followed by an adjective or adverb.

03 SEE Appeared Questions

Question 1
He wanted the branch (because/ so that/ although/ since) he could make a walking stick.
Answer: so that
Explanation: The second clause indicates the purpose of wanting the branch.
Question 2
(Because/ In order to/ Although/ However) it was set out in a shallow dish the stork could not have a single drop of it.
Answer: Because
Explanation: The shallow dish is the reason the stork could not eat. However is an adverb and cannot join clauses directly.
Question 3
(Before/ As/ Because/ Although) you walk down the street you see everyone looking at their cell phones.
Answer: As
Explanation: As indicates simultaneous time meaning while you walk down the street.
Question 4
(Although/ In spite of/ so that/ however) they were new they persuaded every house owner for donation.
Answer: Although
Explanation: The sentence shows contrast. In spite of is a preposition and requires a noun phrase not a clause.
Question 5
(Although/ Because/ However/ So) a few people become successful it is really difficult to achieve it.
Answer: Although
Explanation: The sentence presents a contrast between the small number of successful people and the difficulty of achieving success.
Question 6
(However/ Therefore/ Because/ Although) they return with unforgettable memories.
Answer: Therefore
Explanation: The sentence shows a result. However is an adverb and needs a semicolon or period.
Question 7
The park authorities make the visitors follow strict rules (so that/ although/ because of/ however) the park is protected.
Answer: so that
Explanation: The rules exist for the purpose of protecting the park.
Question 8
(Unless/ If/ Although/ Because) you do not hurry you will miss the bus.
Answer: If
Explanation: Unless already contains a negative. The correct structure here is If you do not hurry.
Question 9
He could not attend the meeting (because/ although/ so/ therefore) he was ill.
Answer: because
Explanation: Illness is the reason for not attending the meeting.
Question 10
(Either/ Neither/ Both/ Not only) Ram nor Shyam came to the party.
Answer: Neither
Explanation: Neither pairs with nor.

04 Common Mistakes to Avoid

Although he was tired but he kept working. Although he was tired he kept working. Never use although and but together.
Because he was late so he was punished. Because he was late he was punished. Never use because and so together.
He was tired however he kept working. He was tired. However he kept working. However is an adverb requiring a semicolon or period.
Unless you do not study you will fail. Unless you study you will fail. Unless is already negative. Do not add another negative.
Neither Ram or Shyam came. Neither Ram nor Shyam came. Neither pairs with nor.
Either Ram nor Shyam will come. Either Ram or Shyam will come. Either pairs with or.
Both Ram as well as Shyam are here. Both Ram and Shyam are here. Both pairs with and.
No sooner I reached than the train left. No sooner had I reached than the train left. No sooner requires inversion and past perfect.
